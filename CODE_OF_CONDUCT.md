# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical decisions using
evidence and concrete examples. Welcome questions, constructive criticism, and
people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, sexualized attention, and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable. Keep discussion relevant
to the project and respect requests to stop disruptive behavior.

Report concerns privately to carloshm91@gmail.com. The maintainer will review
reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.
Reports and personal information will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, project discussions,
and spaces where someone represents Kubetrol.
